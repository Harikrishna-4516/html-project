About Bangalore

Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is the capital city of Karnataka. It is known as the Silicon Valley of India because of its IT companies. The city is famous for its pleasant climate, beautiful gardens, shopping malls, historical monuments, delicious food, and cricket culture.

Popular Tourist Places

Place Image Description
Lalbagh Botanical Garden Lalbagh Botanical Garden is one of India's oldest botanical gardens and is home to thousands of beautiful plants and flowers.
Vidhana Soudha Vidhana Soudha is the Karnataka State Legislature building. It is famous for its grand architecture and historical importance.
M. Chinnaswamy Stadium M. Chinnaswamy Stadium is one of India's most famous cricket stadiums and is the home ground of the Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB).
